How the island works

Every year each villager plays ten rounds against each of its eight neighbours. In each round both sides secretly choose to share or steal, and food is paid out like this (your food, their food):

They share
They steal
You share
3, 3
0, 5
You steal
5, 0
1, 1

Staying alive costs food every year. A villager surrounded by people who share easily covers that cost. One surrounded by thieves starves. When a patch of land opens up, a well-fed neighbour has a child there (the better a villager ate this year, the better its odds), and the child usually inherits the parent's strategy (the mutation slider sets how often it doesn't). Villagers die of old age between 25 and 45. Mistakes is the chance any single move comes out the opposite of what was intended, which is what breaks strategies that never forgive. Newcomers occasionally wash ashore with a random strategy.

The same seed and settings always give the same story, so you can replay a run you liked and record it.
