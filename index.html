A hard mystery RPG. The lighthouse keeper is gone and the sea rises every day. You have 14 days to find the thief, the lookout and the hidden stone, while the guilty lie, evidence gets planted and some puzzles lead nowhere. Every game is a new mystery.

Cheaters & Cooperators

About 3,000 villagers on an island each choose whether to share food with their neighbours or steal it, using one of six strategies they pass on to their children. Trigger droughts or land a boat of Cheaters, and a narrator tells the story of who takes over.

Simulations

10,000 Lives

One person, Sam, lives from 18 to the end, 10,000 times over, with ten big life choices picked at random. A narrator then works out which choices actually mattered for money, happiness and lifespan, and how much came down to luck.

Hares and foxes on a little island, with seasons, snow and real genes for speed, hearing and winter coats. Watch boom-and-bust cycles and evolution happen, with a narrator that picks out the chases and close escapes.
