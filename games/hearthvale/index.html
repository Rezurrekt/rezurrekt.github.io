      How to play

      Days and time. You have 14 days until the Spring Fair. Each day has four parts: morning, midday, afternoon and evening. Story scenes, chats and mini-games use up time; walking, shopping and gardening don't. A yellow marker shows where a story scene is waiting. Go to bed at the cottage to end the day.

      The garden. Click the farm, then Tend the garden. Crops beside each other help or hurt as they grow, so plan your rows. Quality stars raise the price and impress the fair judges. Watch for aphids on day 7.

      Money. Buy seeds at Finch's store, sell produce there any day, or run a stall at the Saturday market, where reading your customers pays better.

      Friends. Chat once a day for a heart, and give gifts; everyone has a favourite crop. Friends bring their families to the fair.

      Saving. The game saves at the end of each day, in this browser. At the end of the book you get a save code that the next book will read.
